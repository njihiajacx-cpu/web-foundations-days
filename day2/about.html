About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple web app for jotting down ideas, reminders and to-dos the moment they come to mind. It keeps your notes in one tidy list so nothing important slips away.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your note in the "New note" box on the Home page.
  2. Click "Add Note" or press Enter.
  3. Find your note in the "Your notes" list below the form.

Features

  • Add notes in one click
  • See how many notes you have at a glance
  • Clean, distraction-free layout
  • Works on phones and computers

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Enter Add the note
Tab Move to the next field or button
